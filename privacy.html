Privacy Policy

Template prepared for a student project. Have it reviewed by a legal adviser before a real-world launch.

What we collect

Donors: name, mobile number, blood group, area, last donation date (optional) and consent. Blood requests: patient name, blood group, units, hospital and the requester’s mobile number. We also store one-time verification codes briefly (as a hash) and basic technical logs.

Why we use it

Only to connect people who need blood with donors who can help, to verify donors by OTP, and to show blood bank stock. We never sell data and never sell blood.

Who can see what

Other users see a donor’s first name and last initial, blood group, area and availability. A donor’s mobile number is never shown publicly. When someone sends a contact request or an emergency request, the donor receives the requester’s number by SMS and decides whether to call. A blood request page is public to anyone with its link and shows the group, units, hospital and the requester’s number, so donors can respond.

Security and retention

We use OTP verification, rate limiting, input validation and access-controlled admin tools. We keep donor and request data until you ask us to delete it or the service is closed.

Your choices

You can ask to correct or delete your profile or a request at any time through the “Partner with us” form on the website (write “data request” in the message). DonorHub is for people aged 18 and over.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date on this page when we do.
