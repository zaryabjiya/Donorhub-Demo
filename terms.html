What DonorHub is

DonorHub is a platform that connects people who need blood with willing donors and shows blood bank stock. We are not a hospital, blood bank or medical provider, and we do not guarantee that a donor or blood will be available.

Medical decisions

Eligibility guidance on this site is general information. The blood bank’s doctor makes the final decision on whether anyone can donate or receive blood. In an emergency, contact the hospital blood bank directly.

No sale of blood

Blood is never bought or sold through DonorHub. Do not ask for or offer payment for blood.

Your responsibilities

You must be 18 or older and give accurate information. Do not post false or repeated requests, harass donors, or misuse contact details. We may remove profiles or requests that break these rules.

Donors

Donating is voluntary. You consent to be contacted when your blood group may be needed and can ask us to delete your profile at any time.

Liability

DonorHub is provided as is. To the extent permitted by law, we are not responsible for outcomes of connections made through the platform.

Changes

We may update these terms and will change the date on this page when we do.
